In its Q2 2025 engineering blog, Northwind said 40% of pull requests on the payments team were drafted by the assistant. The same post says 12% of incidents on that team were opened by the assistant during Q2 2025. Both figures are limited to the payments team. The post does not give a company-wide rate. The author concludes that human engineers are no longer needed. Next year, the assistant will draft 90% of pull requests across the whole company.

The blog is careful about the team and the quarter, and then the closing lines are not. "No longer needed" is the author's view of the 40% figure, not a measurement. The 90% line names a future period and a wider group than the post measured. A claim that drops "payments team" or "Q2 2025" is a different claim from the one the blog made. The two percentages are also separate facts: one is about pull requests, and the other is about incidents.

Someone checking the post should keep those two measurements apart and should keep the payments-team limit on both. The company-wide 90% sentence is about next year, so it is not another Q2 result. The remark that engineers are no longer needed is the author's conclusion, not a third percentage. The post gives no figure for any team except payments.
